Mission Mausam is transforming India's observing network

Under the Ministry of Earth Sciences' Mission Mausam, India is modernising and expanding its weather-observing infrastructure. A central component is a major expansion of the national Doppler Weather Radar network with modern dual-polarization radars across the country.

From space, satellite missions complement these ground-based observations: precipitation radars and radiometers on GPM, India's INSAT geostationary imagers, Oceansat-3 scatterometer winds, the EarthCARE cloud-profiling Doppler radar, and proposed missions such as WIVERN for global in-cloud winds. Together they give a seamless view of clouds, precipitation and winds from storm to global scale.
